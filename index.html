What it checks

  • Title: length, word count, repeated words, “best”-style banned claimsEtsy rule
  • Tags: how many slots you left empty, how many are single words, duplicatesEtsy rule
  • Photos: cover image under 2000px, missing alt text, wrong aspect ratioEtsy rule
  • Copy that reads like AI: “crafted with remarkable attention to detail”Buyer turnoff
  • Description: missing return policy, empty fields, unproven claimsRisk
  • A plain-English rewrite of the specific lines that read as machine-writtenFix

What it doesn’t do

No shop connection. It never asks for access to your store or your Etsy tokens.
No scraping. It doesn’t fetch competitor listings, so it can’t get blocked and can’t break.
No AI-written listings. Buyers scroll past AI-sounding copy. This tells you what to fix. It doesn’t hand you slop.
Runs locally. Your listing text never leaves your browser.
Plain answers. Not a “7/10” score. “Your tag slot 6 repeats a word from slot 2”.

Questions

Is it a Chrome extension?
Yes. It works on any listing you paste in. It also works directly on the listing page you’re already viewing.
Will it connect to my Etsy shop?
No. That’s deliberate. Connecting your shop is the single biggest reason people don’t try tools like this.
How much will it cost?
Planned around $7/month, with the option to cancel in one click. If you only need it for a week, you pay for a week.
When does this ship?
Building it now. The waitlist is how I decide what to build first. If there’s no demand, there’ll be no launch.
